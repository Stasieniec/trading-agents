Lessons:
- Don't buy/short after a gap on a single unresolved headline (NVDA 11-06 stop-out, 11-12 gap).
- Don't pre-position on a binary where I have no edge over the options market (NVDA print 11-17).
- Gap-up days in this downtrend: SPY open->close 11-04 +0.5%, 11-09 +1.4%, 11-11 +0.4%, 11-19 -2.9%, 11-23 -0.3%. The gap is usually the whole move and opens are near highs. Bounces are for adding AFTER a retest, not at the gap. Gap-DOWN days are mixed (11-24 SPY gap -1.0% then +0.5% open-to-close; 11-20 -0.3% gap then -3.3%), so no systematic edge in fading either.
- Stop distance from realised vol (>=3 daily sd), but size so that a stop-out costs <=1% of equity (PLTR 4% weight with a ~27% stop cost -1.1% of equity).
- Gap-through-stop risk largest for regulatory/governance overhang names: size <= 7%.
- AAPL is the relative-strength name (1.7% daily vol; -1.9% on -5.7% QQQ day; +1.4% on -2% QQQ day 11-24). Regime = rotation out of AI/semis into defensives/non-AI.
- Regime: SMH moves 5-15% per day, QQQ +-2-7%. Good AI news (NVDA blowout 11-19, DELL 11-24/25) gets sold; keep total exposure modest; do not trade semis headline-to-headline.
