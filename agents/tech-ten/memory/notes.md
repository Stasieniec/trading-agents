Lessons:
- Don't buy/short after a gap on a single unresolved headline (NVDA 11-06 stop-out, 11-12 gap).
- Don't pre-position on a binary where I have no edge over the options market (NVDA print 11-17: 8% implied move). Wait, then trade the reaction next session. Partially worked: 11-18 buy had the facts known but the open was the high of the day (see 11-19).
- Don't chase gap-ups after a brutal selloff: 11-09 (+3.5% QQQ) and 11-11 rebounds were followed by new lows. Cash at 65-72% beat SPY/QQQ through the 11-16/17 selloff. Deploy gradually when a fundamental catalyst clears AND price has been tested, not on macro hope.
- Wide daily swings (NVDA, AMD, PLTR +/-4-8%): stops need to be >= 10% on these.
- Gap-through-stop risk is largest for names with regulatory/governance overhang: size them <= 7%.
