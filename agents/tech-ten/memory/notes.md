Lessons:
- Don't buy/short after a gap on a single unresolved headline (NVDA 11-06 stop-out, 11-12 gap).
- Don't pre-position on a binary where I have no edge over the options market (NVDA print 11-17: 8% implied move). Wait, then trade the reaction/clarity next session. This worked: on 11-18 I could buy with the beat/raise known and a premarket move well below the implied move.
- Cash at 72% beat SPY/QQQ through the 11-16/17 selloff. Deploy gradually when a fundamental catalyst clears, not on macro hope.
- Wide daily swings (NVDA, AMD, PLTR +/-4-8%): stops need to be >= 10% on these.
- Gap-through-stop risk is largest for names with regulatory/governance overhang: size them <= 7%.
