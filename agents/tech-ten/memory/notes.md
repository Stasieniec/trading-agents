Lessons:
- Don't buy/short after a gap on a single unresolved headline (NVDA 11-06 stop-out, 11-12 gap).
- Don't pre-position on a binary where I have no edge over the options market (NVDA print 11-17).
- Gap-up days in this downtrend: SPY open->close 11-04 +0.5%, 11-09 +1.4%, 11-11 +0.4%, 11-19 -2.9%, 11-23 -0.3%. The gap is usually the whole move and opens are near highs (NVDA 11-18 fill 159.37; MSFT/AMZN fills 11-23 were above close). Bounces are for fading/adding AFTER a retest, not at the gap. Starters small; add only after a close above the open AND a held retest.
- Stop distance from realised vol (>=3 daily sd), but size so that a stop-out costs <=1% of equity. PLTR 4% weight with a ~27% stop cost -1.1% of equity.
- Gap-through-stop risk largest for regulatory/governance overhang names: size <= 7%.
- AAPL is the relative-strength name (1.7% daily vol, -1.9% on a -5.7% QQQ day).
- Regime now: SMH moves 10-15% per day, QQQ +-3-7%. Keep total exposure modest; do not trade the semis headline-to-headline (NVDA +31% then -6% premarket).
