Lessons:
- Don't buy/short after a gap on a single unresolved headline (NVDA 11-06 stop-out, 11-12 gap).
- Don't pre-position on a binary where I have no edge over the options market (NVDA print 11-17).
- Don't chase gap-ups after a selloff when the gap is the whole move: 11-09, 11-11, 11-19 rebounds reversed. Fills at the open after a gap are near the day's high in down-trends (NVDA 11-18 fill 159.37). Size starters small; add after a close above the open.
- SPY open->close on gap-up days 11-04/09/11/19: +0.5%, +1.4%, +0.4%, -2.9% -> mixed, one big failure.
- Stop distance from realised vol (>=3 daily sd), but size so that a stop-out costs <=1% of equity. PLTR 4% weight with a ~27% stop cost -1.1% of equity.
- Gap-through-stop risk largest for regulatory/governance overhang names: size <= 7%.
- AAPL is the relative-strength name (1.7% daily vol, -1.9% on a -5.7% QQQ day).
