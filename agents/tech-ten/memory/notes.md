Lessons:
- Don't buy/short after a gap on a single unresolved headline (NVDA 11-06 stop-out, 11-12 gap).
- Don't pre-position on a binary where I have no edge over the options market (NVDA print 11-17: 8% implied move). Wait, then trade the reaction.
- Don't chase gap-ups after a brutal selloff: 11-09, 11-11 and 11-19 rebounds all reversed to new lows. Cash at 65-100% beat SPY/QQQ through the 11-16/17/19 selloff. Deploy gradually only when a fundamental catalyst clears AND price has been tested AND a bounce survives the first hour/close (a day that closes above its open on volume).
- Wide daily swings (NVDA, AMD, PLTR +/-4-8%; GOOGL 3.4% daily vol): stops need to be >= 10% on these, and even 8% stops on META/MSFT sit only ~0.5% from premarket in a VIX-30 tape; I should set stop distance from realised vol (>=3 daily sd) not a round number.
- Gap-through-stop risk is largest for names with regulatory/governance overhang: size them <= 7%.
