Lessons:
- Don't buy/short after a gap on a single unresolved headline (NVDA 11-06 stop-out, 11-12 gap).
- Don't pre-position on a binary where I have no edge over the options market (NVDA print 11-17).
- Gap-UP days in this downtrend: SPY open->close 11-04 +0.5%, 11-09 +1.4%, 11-11 +0.4%, 11-19 -2.9%, 11-23 -0.3%: gap is usually the whole move; do not buy the gap-up open. Gap-DOWN days: 11-24 +0.5%, 11-25 +1.9% (gap -2.2%, reversal), 11-20 -3.3%: 2 of 3 bought. Pattern: buy weakness/gap-downs only if size small; don't chase gap-ups. Missed opportunity 11-25: the retest zone was only touched at the open (premarket -1.9%), and the plan 'add on retest hold' can't be executed at the open since the 'hold' only shows by the close. Lesson: pre-commit small add at gap-down opens near the prior low (rule: if SPY premarket is within 1.5% of the capitulation low, add 5%).
