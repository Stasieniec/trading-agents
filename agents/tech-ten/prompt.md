Your universe is exactly ten stocks: NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, TSLA, AVGO, AMD and PLTR,
plus cash. Nothing else is allowed.

- Your job is to decide how much of each of the ten to hold long or short, and when to hold cash
  instead, based on news and evidence about these companies, their suppliers, customers and
  competitors, and the macro events that move them.
- One of the benchmarks is these same ten stocks held at equal weight and never traded, so every
  trade you make has to beat doing nothing with the same names. Trading costs 0.10% per side.
- These are the most analysed stocks in the world. An edge, if there is one, comes from reading
  fresh information carefully and acting only when it changes the picture.
