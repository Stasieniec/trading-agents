Lessons (cite trades):
1. Beat-and-raise drift longs fail when macro beta dominates (NVDA, P, ESLT 11-17 to 11-20; ROST/INTU also sold off on beats). Do not add them while QQQ is making new lows; wait for a higher low and falling VIX. In AI hardware, even blowout beats are sold when margins are guided down (DELL, HPE 11-24/25).
2. Honour stops in a rout; do not override (NVDA: the stop saved ~11 more points). But size stops to the vol regime: with SPY moving 3-5%/day an 8-10% stop on a 4-6% daily-vol stock sits inside 2 sigma. AS's stop was widened to 10% on 11-23; AS (3% daily vol, consumer) has held up better than tech.
3. Gap risk: a stop is not a guarantee; NVDA filled -10.0% vs -9.1% stop, P -12.2% vs ~-12% planned.
4. Pre-market strength in a bear tape is not tradable (CRWV 11-11; QQQ +2.1% pre then -2.2% on 11-19). The 11-23 +4.7% rebound faded the next day. Cash is winning versus SPY (equity ~-2.1% vs SPY ~-8% since 10-29), so cash needs a real edge to be replaced.
5. A long beats SPY only with idiosyncratic alpha; a beta bounce bet is a coin flip (about 0.5).
6. Retail earnings include IEEPA tariff-refund benefits - use adjusted numbers (LOW, WSM, TGT, ANF, HPQ).
7. Sympathy gap-ups are not trades. Thin pre-market prints (<1% of avg volume) are unreliable; check shares traded and the IEX last trade.
8. Merger arb: check the spread (ZS vs CRWD ~1.3%; INSM 2.4% for 6 months).
9. No-chasing rule scorecard: ANF closed 11-24 at 168.1 (+19.7%) vs pre-market ~+10.9%, so chasing would have worked on day 1; DKS faded from pre +16.7% to +14.6% close, then flat. Consumer/retail beats are acting as a haven versus AI tech. URBN shows soft guides are punished hard (-12%). Check where DKS/ANF are in a week before changing the rule.
