Lessons (cite trades):
1. Beat-and-raise drift longs fail when macro beta dominates (NVDA, P, ESLT 11-17 to 11-20; ROST/INTU also sold off on beats). Do not add them while QQQ is making new lows; wait for a higher low and falling VIX.
2. Honour stops in a rout; do not override (NVDA: the stop saved ~11 more points). But stops must be sized to the vol regime: with SPY moving 3-5%/day, an 8-10% stop on a 4-6% daily-vol stock sits inside 2 sigma. Size smaller (3-4%) or use wider stops with smaller weight. AS's old stop of 28.06 was 0.7% below the close; widened to 10% on 11-23.
3. Gap risk: a stop is not a guarantee; NVDA filled -10.0% vs -9.1% stop, P -12.2% vs ~-12% planned, because of pre-market gaps.
4. Pre-market strength in a bear tape is not tradable (CRWV 11-11; QQQ +2.1% pre then -2.2% on 11-19). The 11-23 +4.7% rebound faded -1.5% pre the next day, confirming it. Cash is winning versus SPY (equity ~-2.3% vs SPY ~-7% since 10-29), so cash needs a real edge to be replaced.
5. A long beats SPY only with idiosyncratic alpha; a beta bounce bet is a pure market-timing coin flip (about 0.5 probability).
6. Retail earnings include IEEPA tariff-refund benefits - use adjusted numbers (LOW, WSM, TGT, ANF).
7. Sympathy gap-ups (cyber peers after PANW, SPXC etc.) are not trades. Thin pre-market prints (<1% of avg volume) are unreliable.
8. Merger arb on all-stock deals or cash deals: check the spread before getting excited (ZS vs CRWD ~1.3%; INSM 2.4% for 6 months).
9. In 11-24 the digest lists many large pre-market moves on <1% of volume (ETR, AMH, ADI at 1.6%): always check shares traded and the IEX last trade; ADI IEX trade 334 confirmed the drop.
